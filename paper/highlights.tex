\section*{Research Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Four packet-level attacker actions are modeled as Lie-cone generators on flows.
    \item Trajectories in the cone provably preserve rate, length, header and MTU invariants.
    \item On full IDS2018/2017/UNSW-NB15, FFE evades 99.5/99.2/57.6\% of detected attacks.
    \item FFE beats seven baselines; a projected white-box tree attack matches or beats it.
    \item Adversarial training helps at its training budget but much less at larger budgets.
\end{itemize}
