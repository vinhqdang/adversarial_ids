\section*{Research Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Four packet-level attacker actions are modeled as Lie-cone generators on flows.
    \item Trajectories in the cone provably preserve rate, length, header and MTU invariants.
    \item FFE cuts perturbation cost 7,500$\times$ vs PSO with 2.3$\times$ fewer queries.
    \item Physical feasibility remains 93\%--100\%, while baseline PFRs drop to 0\%--15\%.
    \item Dual defense (MPS + LGAT) slashes FFE success from 51.6\% to 5.3\% at M=5.
\end{itemize}
