\section*{Research Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Four packet-level attacker actions are modeled as Lie-cone generators on flows.
    \item Trajectories in the cone provably preserve rate, length, header and MTU invariants.
    \item On full CSE-CIC-IDS2018/2017 and UNSW-NB15, FFE evades 100/96/62\% of attacks.
    \item Feature-space baselines such as HopSkipJump yield 0\% physically feasible flows.
    \item Lie-guided adversarial training cuts FFE success from 96.3\% to 33.0\%.
\end{itemize}
