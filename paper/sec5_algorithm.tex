\subsection{Algorithmic Realization of Flow-Field Evasion}
\label{sec:algorithm}

Algorithm~\ref{alg:ffe_master} gives the master loop of Flow-Field Evasion (FFE). Starting from a flagged flow $x_0 \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{flow}}$ with $F(x_0) \ge 0$, each iteration probes the four generators, takes the admissible step with the largest score reduction per unit cost, and falls back to boundary snapping or temporal dilation when no generator lowers the score.

\begin{algorithm}[!t]
\footnotesize\linespread{1}\selectfont
\caption{Flow-Field Evasion (FFE) Master Optimization Loop}
\label{alg:ffe_master}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Classifier $F$, initial flow $x_0 \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{flow}}$, schema $\mathcal{S}$, step budget $M$, costs $\vec{c}=[c_1,\dots,c_4]^T$, threshold $\tau=0$
\ENSURE Evasive flow $x^* \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{flow}}$ with $F(x^*)<\tau$, or FAILURE
\STATE $x \leftarrow \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}(x_0)$; $f \leftarrow F(x)$; $q \leftarrow 1$
\FOR{$\text{step}=1$ \TO $M$}
    \IF{$f<\tau$} \RETURN $(x,\text{SUCCESS})$ \ENDIF
    \STATE $\mathcal{D}, q_{\text{used}} \leftarrow \text{EstimateLieDerivatives}(F,x,\mathcal{S})$; $q \leftarrow q+q_{\text{used}}$ \COMMENT{Supplementary Algorithm~S1}
    \STATE $k^* \leftarrow$ first maximizer of $(-\nabla_k)/c_k$ over $(k,\nabla_k,\vec{v}_k,\Delta s_k,f_k)\in\mathcal{D}$ with $\nabla_k<0$; null if none
    \IF{$k^* \neq \text{null}$}
        \STATE $x \leftarrow \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}(x+\Delta s_{k^*}\,\vec{v}_{k^*})$; $f \leftarrow f_{k^*}$ \COMMENT{Cached probe, no extra query}
    \ELSE
        \STATE $x_{\text{snap}} \leftarrow \text{TSBS}(F,x,\mathcal{S})$
        \IF{$F(x_{\text{snap}})<f$}
            \STATE $x \leftarrow x_{\text{snap}}$; $f \leftarrow F(x_{\text{snap}})$
        \ELSE
            \STATE $\Delta t \leftarrow \min(1.1\times10^8,\max(5\times10^4,1.5\,x_6))$ \COMMENT{Fallback dilation; projection clamps duration at the $1.1\times10^8\,\mu$s timeout}
            \STATE $x \leftarrow \mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}(x+\Delta t\,\vec{v}_{\text{time}}(x))$; $f \leftarrow F(x)$; $q \leftarrow q+1$
        \ENDIF
    \ENDIF
\ENDFOR
\IF{$f<\tau$} \RETURN $(x,\text{SUCCESS})$ \ELSE \RETURN $(x,\text{FAILURE})$ \ENDIF
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

Supplementary Algorithm~S1 estimates the directional derivatives by probing the ensemble once per generator, since $\nabla F$ vanishes almost everywhere. The probe steps are $\Delta s_1=\max(1000,\,0.5x_6)$ $\mu$s, $\Delta s_2=\max(50,\,0.25x_4)$ bytes, $\Delta s_3=\max(1,\,\text{round}(0.5x_2))$ packets and $\Delta s_4=|64240-x_7|$, and the derivatives are taken per second of delay, per byte, per packet and per window unit of $\Delta s_4/65{,}535$, respectively. Every displacement is followed by the closed-form projection $\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}$ of Supplementary Algorithm~S2, which restores immutable features, enforces monotonicity and integer counts, bounds header and packet lengths, and recomputes the coupled rates.

\subsubsection{Tree-Split Boundary Snapping (TSBS)}
Algorithm~\ref{alg:tsbs} implements Tree-Split Boundary Snapping (TSBS), whose candidates are generated by Supplementary Algorithm~S3. Because the score is piecewise constant in each split feature, a small displacement cannot cross a split threshold. In Tier~1 white-box settings, TSBS therefore extracts the active split thresholds of the ensemble, keeps per feature the threshold closest to the benign side of the current value, and retains the projected candidate that most decreases $F$.

GBDTs also split on coupled telemetry features, so TSBS inverts their constraints in closed form. To cross an active byte-rate threshold $\theta_8$ or packet-rate threshold $\theta_9$, it lengthens the flow duration to
\begin{equation}
    x_6^* = \frac{10^6 (x_4 + x_5)}{\theta_8} + \epsilon_6 \quad \text{or} \quad x_6^* = \frac{10^6 (x_2 + x_3)}{\theta_9} + \epsilon_6,
\end{equation}
with $\epsilon_6 = 10\,\mu$s; the candidate is discarded if $x_6^*$ exceeds the $1.1 \times 10^8\,\mu$s cap. To cross a forward mean packet length threshold $\theta_{10}$ from above, TSBS inverts $x_{10} = (x_4 + N L_0)/(x_2 + N)$ and adds the smallest integer chaff count that satisfies the inequality,
\begin{equation}
    N_{\text{chaff}} = \left\lfloor \frac{x_4 - \theta_{10} x_2}{\theta_{10} - L_0} \right\rfloor + 1 \qquad (L_0 < \theta_{10} < x_{10}).
\end{equation}
For TCP flows, TSBS also snaps the window advertisement toward the benign OS baseline $x_7^* = 64{,}240$ and toward adjacent split partitions.

\begin{algorithm}[!t]
\footnotesize\linespread{1}\selectfont
\caption{Tree-Split Boundary Snapping (TSBS)}
\label{alg:tsbs}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Classifier $F$, flow candidate $x \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{flow}}$ with $F(x) \ge \tau$ (still flagged malicious), feature schema $\mathcal{S}$, tolerance $\epsilon_{\text{rel}}$
\ENSURE Snapped flow $x^* \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{flow}}$
\STATE $x^* \leftarrow \text{copy}(x)$
\STATE $\text{active\_splits} \leftarrow \text{ExtractActiveSplits}(F, x^*)$
\STATE $\mathcal{C} \leftarrow \text{GenerateCandidates}(x^*, \text{active\_splits}, \epsilon_{\text{rel}})$ \COMMENT{Boundary-crossing pool (Supplementary Algorithm~S3)}
\STATE \COMMENT{Evaluate projected candidates and retain best partition crossing}
\STATE $x_{\text{best}} \leftarrow x^*$; $s_{\text{best}} \leftarrow F(x^*)$
\FOR{\textbf{each } $x_{\text{c}} \in \mathcal{C}$}
    \STATE $x_{\text{proj}} \leftarrow \text{ProjectManifoldInvariants}(x_{\text{c}}, \mathcal{S})$
    \IF{$F(x_{\text{proj}}) < s_{\text{best}}$}
        \STATE $s_{\text{best}} \leftarrow F(x_{\text{proj}})$; $x_{\text{best}} \leftarrow x_{\text{proj}}$
        \IF{$s_{\text{best}} < 0$}
            \STATE \textbf{break} \COMMENT{Early exit once benign classification is achieved}
        \ENDIF
    \ENDIF
\ENDFOR
\RETURN $x_{\text{best}}$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsubsection{Query Accounting}
Executing all $M_{\max}=20$ iterations without early termination costs at most $Q_{\max} \le 4M_{\max}+1 = 81$ model queries for the baseline query and Lie-derivative probes, or $\le 101$ if stagnation fallbacks fire, plus the TSBS candidate evaluations, which are not covered by this bound; UDP flows and TCP flows whose window already equals the benign value skip window morphing and cost $q=3$ probes per iteration. In practice, the query count depends on how close the flows sit to a split boundary that a forward generator can cross: on the full benchmarks the mean is 16.5, 14.6 and 93.7 queries per flow against XGBoost on CSE-CIC-IDS2018, CIC-IDS2017 and UNSW-NB15 (Table~\ref{tab:master_evasion_benchmark}), and 14.4 queries on the balanced ablation cohort. We count every probe and every TSBS candidate evaluation as a query to the target, although a Tier~1 adversary could evaluate the candidate snaps offline on its local copy. The score probes themselves need no threshold: FFE without TSBS (Section~\ref{sec:ablation}) queries only the soft score of the target. Because $K \le 4$ and $M \le 20$ are constants, each iteration costs $\mathcal{O}(T \cdot D)$ tree evaluations, and Supplementary Table~S10 compares this asymptotic cost with exact MILP and heuristic search.
