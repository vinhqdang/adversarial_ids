\subsection{Algorithmic Realization: FFE-v2}
\label{sec:algorithm}

FFE-v1 took one fixed-size probe per generator and iteration (Supplementary Algorithm~S1). Validation diagnostics (Supplementary Section~S3) showed where that stalls: on UNSW-NB15 the score plateaus until splits on the packet count, mean length and packet rate are crossed jointly, which needs payload-free packets in magnitudes of tens to hundreds, and elsewhere the single window target lay on the wrong side of the model's borders. FFE-v2 changes five things (Algorithm~\ref{alg:ffev2}); all other machinery, in particular the projection $\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}$ that follows every move (Supplementary Algorithm~S2), is unchanged.

\begin{algorithm}[!t]
\footnotesize\linespread{1}\selectfont
\caption{FFE-v2 (score-only)}
\label{alg:ffev2}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Score oracle $p(\cdot)$ of the target, flow $x_0\in\mathcal{M}_{\text{flow}}$ with $p(x_0)\ge0.5$, generators $\mathcal{G}=\{\text{time},\text{pad},\text{chaff},\text{seg},\text{win}\}$, ladder ratio $r=6$, iteration budget $M=8$, relax-after $R=3$, refinement probes $B=4$, window pool $\mathcal{W}$
\ENSURE Flow $x^*\in\mathcal{M}_{\text{flow}}$ with $p(x^*)<0.5$, or FAILURE
\STATE $x\leftarrow\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}(x_0)$; $z\leftarrow\operatorname{logit}p(x)$
\FOR{$t=1$ \TO $M$}
    \STATE $\mathcal{C}\leftarrow\{\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}(\text{apply}(x,g,a)) : g\in\mathcal{G}\setminus\{\text{win}\},\ a\in\text{Ladder}_g(x)\}\ \cup\ \{\mathcal{P}_{\mathcal{M}}(x\text{ with }x_7{=}w) : w\in\mathcal{W}\}$ \COMMENT{duplicates removed}
    \STATE score all candidates (one query each)
    \IF{some candidate has $p<0.5$}
        \STATE $(g,a,x')\leftarrow$ cheapest crossing candidate under $\mathcal{C}(x_0,\cdot)$; \textbf{break}
    \ENDIF
    \STATE $\Delta_c\leftarrow z-\operatorname{logit}p(c)$ for each candidate; keep those with $\Delta_c\ge10^{-3}$
    \STATE select $\arg\max\ \Delta_c/(\Delta\mathcal{C}_c+10^{-4})$ if $t\le R$, else $\arg\max\Delta_c$ (also if no efficient candidate exists); \textbf{stop with FAILURE if none}
    \STATE $x\leftarrow$ selected candidate; $z\leftarrow\operatorname{logit}p(x)$
\ENDFOR
\IF{a crossing $(g,a,x')$ was found}
    \STATE bisect $a$ with $B$ further probes of the parent state; keep the smallest magnitude that is still scored benign
    \RETURN $x^*$
\ENDIF
\RETURN FAILURE
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Multi-scale probing.} Every generator is probed on a geometric ladder of integer magnitudes $a_k=\min(a_0r^k,\,a_{\max})$ that spans its whole feasible range: delay from 1~ms to the 110~s cap, padding from 16~B to the MTU headroom, one to 4,096 chaff or segmented packets. A plateau of the piecewise-constant score is therefore crossed within one iteration, and identical projected candidates are probed once.

\paragraph{Data-driven window targets.} The window generator tries the six most frequent benign TCP windows of the attacker's benign sample (the training split), in both directions, instead of 64,240~B. A variant with generic operating-system defaults (8,192; 14,600; 29,200; 64,240; 65,535) needs no data.

\paragraph{Log-odds progress and selection.} Progress is the decrease of the log-odds of the returned score, a monotone transform of the same score, which does not saturate where float32 probabilities near 1 tie. If any probe crosses 0.5, the cheapest crossing under the operational cost of Section~\ref{sec:cost} ends the search; otherwise the move with the largest decrease per unit of marginal cost is taken, switching to pure score minimization after three iterations or when no move is cost-efficient.

\paragraph{Cost refinement.} After a crossing, four bisection probes shrink the magnitude of the crossing move; each probe is a scored flow, so success is never assumed.

\paragraph{Optional snapping (Tier 1).} When no probe helps, threshold crossings of \emph{all} mutable features (eight per feature and direction) are converted to closed-form generator magnitudes, including exact inversions of the rate and mean thresholds through delay, padding, chaff and segmentation. This is the only use of the trees' thresholds; the headline variant does not snap.

\paragraph{Query accounting and configuration.} Every scored flow, including probes scored in a batch, is one query. Queries are bounded by the iteration budget times the ladder size plus the refinement probes; observed means are 28--38 on every cell except CIC-IDS2017 CatBoost (109), where it exhausts its budget on unreachable flows (Section~\ref{sec:ablation}). All hyper-parameters (ladder ratio, number of levels, window pool size, refinement probes, iteration budget, relax rule) were chosen on a validation split over 58 configurations (Supplementary Table~S1) and frozen before the single test evaluation: the ladder ratio, refinement probes and window pool size are in \texttt{results/ffev2\_config.json}, and the iteration budget $M=8$, the relax rule $R=3$ and the ladder range are the defaults of \texttt{src/attacks/ffe\_v2.py}. Beam search and the option-padding generator gave no gain worth their queries and were not kept.
