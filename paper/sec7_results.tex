Table~\ref{tab:master_evasion_benchmark} compares FFE with the five baselines. Several baselines evade the detector but not with physically valid flows. The Real ASR of ZOO-NIDS, HopSkipJump and CAA/MOEVA falls from 12.0\% to 3.0\%, from 45.0\% to 8.0\% and from 78.0\% to 48.0\%, because their unconstrained mutations decouple byte and packet rates. Naive coordinate descent never evades the detector (0.0\% ASR).

FFE reaches 100.0\% ASR, PFR and Real ASR. At $N = 100$, the 95\% Wilson score interval for Real ASR is [96.3\%, 100.0\%]. Theorem~\ref{thm:invariant_universal} guarantees that continuous trajectories in the Lie cone remain on the relaxed manifold; on the discrete schema the projection after each step enforces the same relations, so PFR measures conformance to the 12-feature schema and is not an independent test of live packets.

FFE needs 12.9 queries per flow on average, against 680.0 for HopSkipJump, 1,240.0 for CAA/MOEVA and 1,850.0 for PSO, which is 53$\times$, 96$\times$ and 143$\times$ fewer queries. FFE evaluates directional Lie derivatives along its four generators rather than searching the 12-dimensional feature space blindly.

FFE processes the 100 flows in 4.37 s, or 43.7 ms per flow, against 420.15 s for PSO, a 96$\times$ speedup in algorithmic runtime. These wall-clock times are dominated by single-row CUDA inference. Repeated runs of the same benchmark gave totals between 1.5 s and 4.4 s, so only order-of-magnitude conclusions should be drawn. The 43.7 ms excludes the network delay that FFE adds to the packet stream.

FFE also has the smallest perturbation, with mean $\bar{D}_{L_1}$ of 0.251, against 0.342 to 0.512 for ZOO-NIDS, HopSkipJump, CAA/MOEVA and PSO. Naive coordinate descent reaches 204.605.

FFE succeeds on every family in Panel A. The dominant generator follows each family's signature. Temporal dilation dominates the two volumetric floods, whose byte and packet rates the trees split on. Padding dominates brute-force authentication, whose fixed packet lengths sit near a split boundary. Chaff injection dominates web exploits, and window morphing dominates infiltration, which moves the window to the standard client value of 64,240, the benign value in our synthetic generator. The unweighted family mean is 11.6 queries and 59.7 ms. The master cohort needs 12.9 queries because it is drawn from the mixed test partition and has a different family mix.

Across the three corpora and both target models, FFE evades every flow with 100.0\% ASR, PFR and Real ASR. Clean accuracy ranges from 99.63\% to 99.97\%, so the targets are strong detectors. Queries range from 6.8 (CIDDS-001, XGBoost) to 17.8 (UNSW-NB15, LightGBM), and latency from 8.4 ms to 66.0 ms. UNSW-NB15 is the costliest corpus and CIDDS-001 the cheapest. CUDA XGBoost is the slowest model on all three corpora because each single-row query pays a host-to-device transfer, and its latencies vary between runs.
