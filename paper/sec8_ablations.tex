\section{What Drives the Attack: Ablations, Cost and Packet-Level Replay}
\label{sec:ablation}

\paragraph{Ablations on the validation split.} Supplementary Table~S1 varies one factor at a time over nine cells. The data-driven window pool is decisive on the CSE-CIC-IDS2018 and CIC-IDS2017 corpora (removing it drops mean Real ASR from 91.6\% to 60.6\%), chaff matters on UNSW-NB15 for LightGBM and CatBoost (59.9\% and 63.9\% without it), and the ladder must span the range. Re-segmentation adds little on validation once chaff and padding exist (96.6\%, 96.9\% and 97.2\% without it against 96.7\%, 97.5\% and 97.4\% on UNSW-NB15), yet 39\%, 16\% and 24\% of FFE-v2's successful UNSW-NB15 flows use it (Supplementary Table~S4).

\paragraph{Cost.} FFE-v2 is not uniformly cheaper than FFE-v1: on UNSW-NB15 its paired median operational cost on the flows both evade is higher (6.7 against 5.8, 14.6 against 7.5 and 29.1 against 9.8 for XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost), and against the control it is cheaper for XGBoost and LightGBM (32.5 against 213 and 35.8 against 49) but not for CatBoost (22.1 against 10.5; Supplementary Tables~S2 and~S3).

\paragraph{Packet-level replay.} To check that the generators correspond to realizable packet operations we expanded the action ledger of each successful flow of the first 300 test flows per corpus (XGBoost) into a synthetic packet trace, re-extracted the twelve features with a CICFlowMeter-style extractor (our re-implementation) and re-scored the target (Supplementary Section~S8, Table~S15). When chaff packets carry the booked bytes, or empty chaff is compensated by padding, 100\% of the replayed flows reproduce the schema vector within 1\%, stay feasible and stay evasive, including the 118 UNSW-NB15 flows that use re-segmentation (replayed ASR 99.3\%, 100.0\% and 98.3\%). If chaff packets are empty and the bytes the schema credits to them are not sent, the easy corpora are unchanged but UNSW-NB15 retains only 69.2\% of its evasions (replayed ASR 68.0\%), the same sensitivity as for FFE-v1. The replay validates the action semantics and the schema's internal consistency only: the traces are synthetic, no TCP state machine or server reaction is modelled (in particular whether more forward segments elicit more server ACKs), and nothing was sent over a network.
